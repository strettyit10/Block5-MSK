How to Play

01 · Learn

Structure Builders

Fill in structures box by box; Drill repeats what you miss.

02 · Test

Boss Gauntlets

One per lecture. Clinical vignettes, solo or 2-player co-op.

03 · Review

Creature Campaigns

RPG mixing every lecture in a subject. Best right before the exam.

04 · Exam-ready · USMLE style

Bell Ringer

Board-style vignettes with gross, X-ray and slide stations. 2-player duel: alternate turns or buzzer race.
